Behind the Pixels

PixelCrypt v2 uses AES-256-GCM encryption with pseudo-random pixel scattering to securely hide data inside images. Generic LSB extraction tools cannot recover the payload.

Binary Injection

Your message is converted to a bitstream. Each bit replaces the lowest bit of an RGB channel.

AES-256-GCM + PRNG Scatter

Data is AES-256-GCM encrypted, then embedded at pseudo-random pixel positions derived from your credential. Generic extractors get noise.

Zero Knowledge

All processing happens locally in your browser. Your images and passwords never touch a server.
